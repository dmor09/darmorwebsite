The Darmor approach

Search should feel more human.

Technology changes fast. The value of judgment, trust, and a conversation that gets beneath the résumé does not.

Who we are

Focused search.
Real partnership.

Founded in New York in 2019, Darmor Partners was built to be the kind of recruiting partner we believed ambitious teams deserved: informed, candid, agile, and invested in what happens after the offer is signed.

We work with companies from emerging startups to Fortune 500 organizations, acting as an extension of their team. For candidates, we are a trusted advisor through one of the most consequential decisions of a career.

How we work

One search.
Seven deliberate moves.

Explore the journey from first conversation to long-term success.

01 · DISCOVER

Define the mission.

We get beneath the job description—aligning on outcomes, technical requirements, team dynamics, culture, and the story that will attract the right person.

Clarity before activity
02 · MAP

Build the universe.

Our researchers map the market, adjacent talent pools, competitors, and high-potential profiles to create a focused search universe.

Research-led search
03 · ENGAGE

Create conviction.

We reach passive talent with context-rich outreach and a compelling, credible narrative—not a generic message sent at scale.

Human, targeted outreach
04 · ASSESS

Go beyond the résumé.

Every conversation explores capability, motivation, trajectory, values, and the environment in which a candidate does their best work.

Fit in both directions
05 · COORDINATE

Keep momentum.

We manage interviews, stakeholder alignment, candidate preparation, and feedback loops so the process stays clear and decisive.

A seamless experience
06 · CLOSE

Navigate the finish.

Market context and candid guidance support the offer, negotiation, resignation, and transition without adding pressure.

Confidence at the close
07 · FOLLOW THROUGH

Stay connected.

We check in throughout the first year to protect retention, surface feedback early, and help the partnership thrive.
